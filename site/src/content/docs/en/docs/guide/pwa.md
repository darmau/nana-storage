Nana is a PWA: add it to your phone's home screen or your computer's dock and it opens in its own window.

- **iPhone / iPad**: open the library in Safari › Share › Add to Home Screen.
- **Android**: Chrome menu › Add to Home screen / Install app.
- **Desktop**: the install icon on the right of the address bar in Chrome or Edge.

In the standalone window:

- **Pull to refresh** when at the top of a page.
- A banner appears when you're offline or your login expired, with a reload button.
- On phones, the tab bar at the bottom has Photos, Albums, Explore, Folders and Library.

There's no service worker, so no photo is ever cached on the device beyond the browser's private cache.
